# Balcona Office — Final Visual Direction Lock

Status: LOCKED
Date: 2026-08-28

## Decision

The Office visual direction is now locked to **Direction A — business-grade Back Office**.

This is a final Office-direction choice, not another A/B/C experiment.

The prototype review produced the strongest behavioral signal with A:
- the interface receded
- the business information became more prominent
- the screen encouraged scanning numbers and operational state rather than admiring UI decoration

That is exactly the desired job of Balcona Office.

## What A means

Office should feel:
- calm
- dense
- professional
- analytical
- operationally credible
- visually quiet enough that numbers, exceptions, tables, and state carry attention

Office should not feel:
- like a marketing dashboard
- like the Guest product
- like a dark premium lounge
- like a generic colorful SaaS template

## Reference proof remains active

The final system still follows the approved evidence stack:

- Lightspeed: Back Office structure, density, business-domain navigation
- Toast: decision hierarchy and management overview
- Square: interaction clarity and operational states
- Oracle Simphony: scope/inheritance discipline
- Balcona: product truth, bilingual hospitality identity, backend capabilities

The decision does **not** discard the research or brief. It narrows the visual execution.

## Balcona identity inside A

Balcona identity remains through:
- name/wordmark
- restrained bronze/copper accent
- warm-neutral rather than cold-blue neutral palette
- hospitality language
- Arabic/RTL quality
- product-specific information and workflows

Do not reintroduce dark brown/glass styling merely to make Office feel more “Balcona”.

## Prototype requirement

The next prototype is one coherent Office product, not multiple visual directions.

It must include a working shell and representative, clickable surfaces for:
- Home
- Operations
- Catalog
- Inventory
- Locations
- Team
- Money
- Insights
- Experience
- Settings

It must preserve:
- All Locations / branch scope
- search/command entry
- attention inbox concept
- Arabic/RTL
- dense tables and list investigation
- detail/action patterns
- normal, exception, and partial-error states where relevant

This lock applies to **Balcona Office** only. Service, Kitchen, Guest, Setup, and Platform retain their context-specific directions from R11.
